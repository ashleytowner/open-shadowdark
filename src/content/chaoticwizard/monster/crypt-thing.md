# Crypt Thing

*Leathery, dry-skinned undead tomb guardian, meant to disperse intruders.*

**AC** 11, **HP** 24, **ATK** 2 claws +4 (1d8) or 1 teleporting burst , **MV** near, **S** +1, **D** +1, **C** +2, **I** +0, **W** +0, **Ch** +0, **AL** C, **LV** 5

**Greater Undead.** Immune to morale checks. Only damaged by silver or magical sources.

**Teleporting Burst.** 1/day, all creatures within near, WIS 15+ or teleported to a random location within far.
