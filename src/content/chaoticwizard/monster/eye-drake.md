# Eye-Drake

*Eyes cover every joint of this scale-less, limb-less dragon’s wings. A giant eye sits within its mouth.*

**AC** 16, **HP** 52, **ATK** 1d4 eyestalk ray, **MV** double near (fly), **S** +3, **D** +0, **C** +3, **I** +2, **W** +2, **Ch** +3, **AL** C, **LV** 11

**Anti-Magic Ray.** All spells are DC 15 to cast when in view of it’s mouth-eye.

**Eyestalk Ray.** Each ray can shoot once per round and target one creature or an object up to 1,000 pounds (up to four on same target).

1. **Lightning.** DC 12 DEX or 4d8 damage (DISADV on check if wearing metal armor).
2. **Fire.** DC 12 DEX or 6d10 damage.
3. **Poison.** DC 12 CON or 3d8 damage.
4. **Freeze.** DC 12 STR or frozen for 1d6 rounds.
5. **Steam.** DC 12 DEX or 4d12 damage.
6. **Smog.** DC 12 CON or blinded for 1d4 rounds.
